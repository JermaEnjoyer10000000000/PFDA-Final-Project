# Description

As the project name suggests, this is a tool used to download videos from the internet to store on your PC/Laptop. Currently, with this iteration of the project, users are capable of pasting the URL into a text box. holding backspace to delete rapidly, downloading one video at a time, and storing the video in it's own dedicated download folder. While not at the best resolution, users are still able to download their video to their PC with audio intact.

Users are provided with a text box for pasting their URL into on the first screen. Clicking on the video info button will provide the title of the video from the URL uploaded into the text box. Clicking the download button will begin the download process and momentarily text will appear at the bottom reading "Download complete!" to let the user know it's done.

During the design of the program, I focused first on nailing the main function of the program over the visual aesthetic of the program. In the future, I'd prefer to spend more time with the GUI. The current GUI as a whole functions properly but lacks any sort of visual appeal. I believe having a progress bar that appears when the download begins and putting more emphasis on the GUI as a whole would improve the experience of the program greatly. Regardless, I feel content with the state of the project and what I have been able to create thus far.
